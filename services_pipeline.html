4 · Backup & monitoring (independent safety net)

These services have no pipeline order — they run independently to protect data and keep the infrastructure the other pipelines rely on (disk space, the rclone mount, the database) healthy.
MySQL Backup
hourlyPHP60 m
Hourly dumps of 4 critical tables, daily for the rest; keeps hourlies 14 days, then one per day for 1 year.
Backup Zin EAF/SRT files
dailyPython90 m
ZIPs all .eaf/.srt from /web/zin/eaf/zin/ to the rclone mount; keeps the last 3 archives.
Check Disk
hourlyPython
Writes root-filesystem stats to diskCheck.json; e-mails an alert via Mailjet when free space drops below 30 %.
Rclone Mount Monitor
hourlyPython5 m
Verifies the “signcollect” rclone mount process is alive and the mount is readable; alerts on FUSE errors or crashes.
Mailjet
e-mail alerts to admin
Scheduler behaviors (scheduler.py): 20-worker thread pool · per-service timeout (default 30 min, configurable) with automatic process kill · stuck “running” services cleaned after 3 h · heartbeat to watchdog.log every 5 min with watchdog alerts after 10 min silence · per-service log file (<service>.log) in its working directory · graceful shutdown on SIGINT/SIGTERM.
